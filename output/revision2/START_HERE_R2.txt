UPPER PRINT SETUP
Print open bottom rim DOWN, palm face UP; already oriented diagonally.
Use 0.12 mm layers, 0.20 mm initial layer, 3 walls, 15% infill, internal tree
supports and 5 mm outer brim. Remove supports through the open underside.
The 167.63 x 163.86 mm model leaves room for the 5 mm brim on A1 mini.
Do not flip palm-down: the outside fillet becomes an unsupported expanding
overhang and can sag badly, as seen in the user's failed print.
The brim increases adhesion area but cannot guarantee bed adhesion. Clean the
plate and confirm a continuous attached first layer before leaving the print.
Bottom: flat underside down, 0.20 mm layers, 2-3 walls, 15% infill, no support.
Retainer: flat bridge face down, legs up, 0.20 mm layers, no support.

ASSEMBLY AND FASTENERS
Drop ring with seated cushion into top recess. Bring mechanism to the underside
of the ledge and insert four M2.5 screws from above into the hardware flange.
Stack through printed ledge and ring is 7.46 mm; select screw length for actual
thread depth (10 mm gives nominal 2.54 mm engagement). The CAD screw envelope
is illustrative; do not blindly use a longer screw that bottoms in hardware.
Cover: four M3 x 10 countersunk screws into 2.5 mm plastic pilots.
Retainer: two M2.5 x 12 pan-head screws into 2 mm plastic pilots. Tighten gently.
Four approximately 12 mm adhesive feet, small cable tie for the harness anchor.
Solder PCB on bench; locate USB at rear; route wires clear of mechanism and lip.
Bridge pressure shoes must bear only on clear PCB edges, not solder pads.
